# How to clear Ableton Live cache on Mac

Quit Live, open Finder, choose Go > Go to Folder, and enter `~/Library/Caches/Ableton`. Move the contents of that folder to the Trash and empty it. Live recreates what it needs the next time you open it. Then check the decoding cache size in Live's settings. Leave your projects, your User Library and your Preferences folder alone.

## What the Ableton cache is

Live stores temporary working files so it does not have to repeat work. The main one is the decoding cache: when you use compressed audio such as MP3 or similar formats, Live decodes the audio and keeps the result on disk. Other small cache files sit in your user Library.

These files are made by Live and can be made again. Deleting them does not touch your music. The cost is that Live may take a little longer the first time it opens a clip that needs decoding again.

## Clear the cache folder by hand

1. Save your work and quit Live with `Live > Quit Live` (or Command-Q).
2. In Finder, choose `Go > Go to Folder…` and enter `~/Library/Caches/Ableton`.
3. Open the folder. You may see subfolders named for Live versions or components.
4. Select the contents and move them to the Trash. You can leave the `Ableton` folder itself in place.
5. Empty the Trash with `Finder > Empty Trash…`.
6. Open Live and load a project to confirm everything works as before.

If you have several Live versions installed, they share this location, so close all of them first.

## Check the decoding cache in Live's settings

Live has its own cache controls, and they are the cleaner route if you want to limit the size going forward.

1. Open Live and choose `Live > Settings…`.
2. Go to the `File & Folder` tab.
3. Find the Decoding Cache section. It shows the cache folder and a maximum cache size.
4. Use the cleanup button there to remove the cache, or lower the maximum size if your drive is tight.

The same tab also lists a temporary folder, used for recordings in progress. Do not empty that folder while Live is open or while a take is being recorded.

## What is safe to delete, and what is not

**Safe to remove (Live makes it again):**

- The contents of `~/Library/Caches/Ableton`
- The decoding cache, through Live's own settings

**Leave alone:**

- `~/Music/Ableton/User Library`: your presets, racks, clips and templates.
- `~/Library/Preferences/Ableton`: your settings, licensing and Options.txt. Deleting it resets Live to a fresh install.
- `~/Library/Application Support/Ableton`: the Live database and other app data.
- Project folders, especially `Samples/Recorded` and `Samples/Processed`. Recordings, freezes and consolidated clips live there, and your set may depend on them.

If you want to reclaim space inside a project, do it from Live rather than in Finder. Open the set, then use `File > Manage Files` and look at Manage Project. Live shows which files the set uses, so you are not guessing.

Old `Backup` folders inside projects hold earlier saves. Check a project opens correctly before you remove them, and keep any backup you may want to go back to.

## FAQ

**Will clearing the cache delete my projects or samples?**
No. The cache is separate from your project folders and User Library. Only remove the cache contents, and keep the folders listed above.

**Will Live behave differently afterward?**
Live works as before. Compressed audio may need to be decoded again the first time you play it, so that first load can take a moment longer.

**How often should I clear it?**
Only when you need the space or when you are troubleshooting. Live manages the cache on its own, and the maximum size in `File & Folder` settings keeps it in check.
